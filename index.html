Fitness to Drive

At-A-Glance Guide for Emergency Medicine (Sept 2025)

🚨 LEGAL DUTY: It is the PATIENT'S duty to notify DVLA, not the doctor's (unless public safety is at immediate risk). Document your advice.
⚖️ The Basics
Risk Thresholds
  • Group 1: >20% risk/year
  • Group 2: >2% risk/year
3-Month Rule
Usually NO notify for conditions lasting < 3 months (e.g., fractures).
🧠 Neurology
Unexplained TLoC
G1: Stop 6 months.
G2: Stop 12 months.
Reflex Syncope
Drive if prodrome reliable & not whilst driving.
Cough Syncope
G1: Stop 6 months.
🧠 Neuro (Cont)
First Unprovoked Seizure
G1: Stop 12 months.
G2: Stop 5 years.
Provoked Seizure
e.g., Hyponatraemia, illness.
G1: Stop 6 months.
Stroke / TIA
TIA: Stop 1m. No notify if no deficit.
Stroke: Stop 1m. Notify if deficit remains.
❤️ Cardiovascular
ACS (MI, NSTEMI)
G1: Stop 1 wk if PCI+EF≥40%. Else 4 wks.
G2: Stop 6 wks min.
Arrhythmia
Incapacitating? Stop. Resume after 4 wks control.
Pacemaker
G1: Stop 1 wk. Notify.
ICD (Defib)
G1: Stop 6 months. Notify.
G2: Permanent Bar.
🩸 Diabetes
Insulin / Severe Hypo
G1: Must have hypo awareness. Max 1 severe hypo in 12m.
G2: Strict monitoring (6wk logs).
Hypo while driving
MUST STOP DRIVING immediately.
💊 Psych & Drugs
Psychosis / Mania
Stop acute. Resume 3m stable (G1).
Alcohol / Drugs
Persistent misuse:
G1: 6 months (Alc/Cannabis) or 1 yr (Opiates).
