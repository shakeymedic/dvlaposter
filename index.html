Fitness to drive

Full DVLA guidance
The patient must tell DVLA. DVLA decides when they can drive.

1. Advise

Tell the patient their condition may affect driving, and whether to stop (see below).

2. Signpost

Ask them to check GOV.UK or tell DVLA, and let DVLA decide.

3. Document

Record what advice you gave, for example:

"Advised not to drive and to tell DVLA about first seizure. Patient understood."

Public safety risk? Patient won't stop driving? Speak to a senior about telling DVLA without consent.

Group 1

Cars, motorcycles, and vans up to 3.5 tonnes (most delivery drivers).

Group 2 stricter rules

Lorries over 3.5 tonnes (C1, C), minibuses and buses (D1, D).

Drives for work? Ask what they drive.

  • Taxi and private hire: councils should apply Group 2 rules.
  • Delivery: van up to 3.5 t is Group 1; larger lorry is Group 2.
  • Paramedics, police, fire: rules for the vehicle they drive (ambulance over 3.5 t is Group 2). Employers may set higher standards.
